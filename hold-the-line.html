Privacy Policy

Hold the Line: Mutant Front · Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy explains how Hold the Line: Mutant Front (the "Game"), published by CanoGames ("we", "us"), handles information when you play on iPhone or iPad.

Summary

We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information. The Game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Information we collect

None. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data, and we do not use advertising identifiers.

Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (for example, your wave record, upgrades and settings such as sound volume) locally on your device using the browser storage built into the app. This data stays on your device. We cannot see it. It is removed if you delete the app.

Third-party services

The iOS version of the Game does not include advertising, analytics or other third-party SDKs and does not make network requests to collect data about you.

Children

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no personal data is collected, no such data can be shared or sold.

What the app stores

Bounce & Break does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location. Your game progress (levels, stars, coins and settings) is stored only on your device. We do not operate a server and we do not receive your progress.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the iOS advertising identifier), approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads.

On first launch iOS asks whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency). If you allow it, ads may be personalized; if you decline, the advertising identifier is not used for personalized ads. Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are also shown a consent form and can change their choice later.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can turn off tracking or reset your advertising identifier at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Deleting the app removes all game data stored on your device.

Data sales

We do not sell your personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page will change when we do.
